Browser Evolution

The Browser Wars

Microsoft vs. Netscape was known as one of the most significant technology battles in history. Netscape had over 80% of the browser market share in 1995. Microsoft saw this as a major threat to Windows. Netscape required users to pay a subscription fee to use their browser. Microsoft took the opposite approach by making Internet Explorer completely FREE and bundling it with the Windows operating system. By 2001, Internet Explorer had over 90% of the browser market share. Microsoft faced an antitrust lawsuit from the US Justice Department for its browser bundling practices.

Modern Browsers

Today, the major browsers are Chrome, Safari, Edge, and Firefox. Each one uses a browser engine to display pages.

Impact of Chromium

Chromium is an open-source browser project that forms the basis for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge, which now control about ~80% of the market. Firefox and Safari have smaller market shares, and are the only non-Chromium browsers remaining. This raises concerns about the lack of browser diversity and the potential for monopolistic practices.
